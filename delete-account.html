CityAura – Account Deletion Request

This page explains how users of CityAura, developed by yannicksd (Yannick Schlagenhauf), can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to yannicksdapps@gmail.com.
  2. Use the email address that is associated with your CityAura account.
  3. Include a request stating that you would like your account to be deleted.
  4. We will respond and ask for verification to confirm that the account belongs to you.
  5. Once verified, we will permanently delete your account and all associated data.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Your account information (including email address).
  • Profile data.
  • Any in-app activity or user-generated data associated with your account.
  • Any other personal data linked directly to your account.

Data Retention Policy

Once your identity is verified and your deletion request is processed, all data associated with your account will be permanently deleted.

We do not retain any personal data after account deletion is completed.

If you have any additional questions regarding your data, please contact yannicksdapps@gmail.com.
